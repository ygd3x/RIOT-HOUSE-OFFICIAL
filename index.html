RIOT HOUSE RH ONE GAMING SYSTEM
01 / A NEW KIND OF CONSOLE

THE POWER IS ON YOUR WRIST.

Traditional gaming consoles sit on a shelf or beneath a television. RH ONE explores a different possibility: putting the gaming system directly on the player. The wearable design is intended to become the central interface for accessing games, managing experiences, and interacting with the RIOT HOUSE ecosystem.
