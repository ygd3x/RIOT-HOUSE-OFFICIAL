03 / THE EQUIPMENT

RH
GEAR.

FEEL MORE. INTERACT DIFFERENTLY.

RH GEAR is the proposed wearable equipment system designed to work alongside RH ONE. The vision includes a gaming vest and interactive gloves that help connect a player's physical movements to digital gameplay.

THE WEARABLE SYSTEM

YOUR BODY BECOMES PART OF THE EXPERIENCE.

Instead of relying only on a traditional controller, RH GEAR explores new ways for players to interact with virtual environments through wearable technology.

01 / RH VEST

The Gaming Vest

A proposed wearable vest designed to support immersive gameplay. Future development could explore haptic feedback, motion tracking, and adjustable fit.

02 / RH GLOVES

Interactive Gloves

Wearable gloves envisioned to detect hand movements and gestures, giving players new ways to interact with objects and controls in a digital world.

ONE CONNECTED SYSTEM
RH ONE + RH GEAR

The wearable console and the equipment are designed to work together as part of one gaming ecosystem.

THE EXPERIENCE

INTERACTION BEYOND THE CONTROLLER.

Movement Recognition

Explore how compatible sensors could recognize body and hand movements and translate them into game actions.

Haptic Feedback

Investigate technology that could provide physical sensations, such as vibrations or other feedback, during gameplay.

Wireless Connection

Develop a reliable way for RH GEAR to communicate with RH ONE and other compatible system components.

Player Comfort

Prioritize comfortable materials, adjustable sizing, safe movement, and equipment that players can wear during extended sessions.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

RH GEAR is a proposed product concept. The vest, gloves, sensors, haptic systems, wireless connections, and software have not yet been engineered or tested as a working product.

THE BIGGER VISION

DON'T JUST CONTROL THE GAME. INTERACT WITH IT.

RH GEAR is one part of the RIOT HOUSE vision. Combined with RH ONE and IMMERSION, it could help create a gaming experience that feels more connected to the player's real movements and surroundings.
