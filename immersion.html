GAMING WITHOUT THE BOUNDARIES OF A SCREEN.

IMMERSION is the RIOT HOUSE vision for transforming the player's physical surroundings into part of a digital gaming experience. Imagine stepping into a game where the walls, floor, and environment around you become part of the world you're exploring.

THE ENVIRONMENT

WHAT IF YOUR ROOM BECAME THE GAME?

The goal is to move beyond looking at a virtual world through a screen. IMMERSION explores room-scale technology that could make a player's surroundings feel like an extension of the game itself.

HOW IT COULD WORK

THE ROOM BECOMES PART OF THE EXPERIENCE.

Wall Projection & Displays

Explore projection or display technologies that could transform walls into changing game environments.

Interactive Flooring

Investigate floor-based displays or other spatial technologies that could make the ground part of the digital environment.

Room Tracking

Explore sensors and spatial mapping that could help the system understand the room, the player, and their movement.

Real-Time Environment

Imagine game environments that respond to player actions and change what appears around them.

THE BIGGER VISION

NOT JUST VIRTUAL REALITY. A NEW WAY TO EXPERIENCE GAMING.

IMMERSION brings together the wearable console, connected equipment, and room-scale technology. The ambition is to make gaming feel less like watching a screen and more like stepping into another world.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

IMMERSION is a technology vision under development. Room-scale displays, projection systems, spatial tracking, floor interaction, safety, and real-time game integration would require substantial research, engineering, and testing. This page describes the concept, not a finished working system.
