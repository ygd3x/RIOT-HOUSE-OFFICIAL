YOUR GAME. ON YOUR WRIST.

RH ONE reimagines what a gaming console can look like. Instead of a traditional console box, the core device is designed to be worn as a watch. It is envisioned as the central hardware of the RIOT HOUSE ecosystem.

RH ONEGAME SYSTEM
THE IDEA

A CONSOLE THAT MOVES WITH YOU.

RH ONE is envisioned as a wearable gaming device that connects the player to games, accessories, and immersive experiences. The goal is to make the console itself part of what the player wears rather than a separate piece of furniture or equipment.

Wearable Hardware

A wrist-worn device designed around gaming controls, computing hardware, connectivity, and a dedicated display.

Gaming Interface

A visual interface concept for accessing games, system settings, connected gear, and player controls.

Connected Ecosystem

RH ONE is intended to act as the central connection point for compatible RIOT HOUSE equipment.

Designed to Expand

The long-term vision is a system that can grow through new hardware, software, and immersive experiences.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

RH ONE is currently a product concept. Its hardware, software, battery system, and technical architecture still need to be researched, designed, engineered, and tested.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

THE START OF SOMETHING BIGGER.

RH ONE is only the beginning. The wearable console is designed to be part of a larger vision involving RH GEAR, a connected control system, and immersive environments that could transform how people experience games.
